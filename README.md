# Huntington Community of Southwestern Ontario — Website Redesign Concept

VVIP redesign concept for Huntington Community of Southwestern Ontario (HCSWO), a Huntington disease support charity rooted in London, Ontario.

7-page concept (What We Do, About Us & HD, Our Team, Families & Caregivers, Resources, Contact, Donate Now) built from the organisation's own content, in their blue/purple/green identity. Contact form and donate widget are demo-only: nothing is sent or charged.

Concept prepared by Francis (mockupbyfrancis.site). Live site: huntingtoncommunity.ca (their current site).
